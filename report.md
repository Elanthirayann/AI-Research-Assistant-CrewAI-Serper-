## Query: what is gate exam 
**Time:** 2026-04-02 14:51:42

- The GATE exam is a national-level exam in India that tests a candidate's knowledge in their undergraduate field of study.
- The exam is conducted jointly by the Indian Institute of Science and seven Indian Institutes of Technology, and is typically held in February every year.
- The GATE score is used for admission to postgraduate programs and as a recruitment tool by several Indian public sector undertakings.
